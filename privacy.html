Privacy Policy

Your data stays with you.

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Awaab is designed so that you never have to trust us with your information — because we never receive any. This page explains exactly what happens, in plain language.

The short version

  • Awaab collects, stores, and transmits no personal data.
  • No account is required to use the app.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no third-party trackers, no crash-reporting services.
  • Location and motion sensors are used only on your phone, and only for the features that need them.
  • The full Quran and all prayer-time calculations are included with the app and run on your phone.

What Awaab does not collect

Awaab does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. There is no account system, no sign-in, and no identifier that ties the app to you. We do not run analytics, we do not show advertising, we do not use any third-party tracker, and we do not use any crash-reporting service. Awaab does not connect to any server of ours — because there isn't one.

Where your data lives

Anything Awaab needs to remember — your preferences, your bookmarks, your selected calculation method, your last known location — is saved on your phone. It is never copied to a server, because Awaab does not operate any server.

On-device sensors

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is used only on your phone to calculate accurate prayer times for where you are, and to determine the direction toward the qibla. Awaab itself never transmits or stores your location off your phone.

One honest detail about translating coordinates into a city name. When you ask Awaab to detect your location, the app may also ask your phone's operating system to translate your coordinates into a city name to display. On iPhone and Android, this lookup is handled by Apple's or Google's own services. That step is performed by your phone's operating system, not by Awaab, and is governed by their own privacy policies. Awaab cannot see or capture what they do with that request.

Compass and motion sensor

The compass and motion sensors are used only on your phone to power the qibla finder. Your heading stays on the phone — it is not transmitted anywhere.

Quran and prayer-time content

The Quran and all prayer-time calculations are fully on your phone. The Quran is included with the app and ready to read at any moment. Prayer times are calculated on your phone using a standard astronomical formula and your location. The app does not make any network request to load the Quran or to fetch prayer times.

Notifications

Awaab can schedule quiet reminders for each prayer time. These reminders are scheduled in advance and delivered entirely by your phone. They are not push notifications from a server — there is no server.

What Apple and Google may collect

When you download or use Awaab through the App Store or Google Play, the platforms themselves may collect standard platform-level information — for example, download counts, device model, operating-system diagnostics, and crash reports they collect on Awaab's behalf at the OS level. This data is governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies and is outside Awaab's control. Awaab does not access this data and does not have any analytics agreement with either platform.

Children

Because Awaab does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. The app is safe to use at any age — see the Age Suitability statement for more.

Changes to this policy

If anything about how Awaab handles data changes, this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change to reflect it. The privacy posture above is a core design commitment of the app — we don't intend to change it.
