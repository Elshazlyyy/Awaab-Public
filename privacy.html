PRIVACY

Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED · 22 MAY 2026

In one line

Awaab does not collect any user data. We do not have analytics, no servers receive your information, and no third-party SDKs are bundled. Everything happens on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no profile. We do not assign you an identifier, we do not record what you read, and we do not track when or how often you open the app.

What is stored on your device

The following data lives only on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the app:

  • Settings — language, theme, calculation method, Asr school, time format, prayer-alert toggles, and Arabic font size.
  • Location coordinates — when granted, used only to compute prayer times. We never transmit your location.
  • City label — derived from your coordinates via on-device reverse-geocoding, or whatever city you manually entered.
  • Last-read position — the sūrah and verse you were reading, so you can resume.
  • Bookmarks — verses you have saved.
  • Cached prayer times — pre-computed for your location, refreshed daily on-device.

None of this is shared with us, with Apple or Google beyond what the OS already does for any installed app, or with anyone else.

Network traffic

Awaab makes no outbound network requests on its own. The exceptions:

  • App Store / Play Store — the OS itself may check for updates. We do not control this.
  • OTA bundle check — when you tap "check for updates" in Settings, we ask our static update server for the latest JavaScript bundle metadata. No personal data is sent, only the current bundle ID.

That is the entire list. We do not contact analytics services, ad networks, crash reporters, or any other third party.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs. The marketing site (elshazlyyy.github.io/Awaab-Public) uses no analytics and no cookies. Google Fonts are served from fonts.googleapis.com; if you would prefer they were self-hosted, please tell us and we will move them.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data the app handles, we will update the date at the top of this page and ship the new policy with the corresponding app release. We will not change this policy silently.
