PRIVACY

Privacy policy

LAST UPDATED · 23 MAY 2026

In one line

Your personal content — location, settings, bookmarks, what you read — never leaves your device. Awaab does transmit a small set of anonymous operational telemetry via Expo Application Services so we can ship bug fixes and understand rough adoption. No name, no email, no advertising identifiers, no sale of data to anyone. The full disclosure is below.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information.
  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profile.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android — explicitly blocked in the manifest).
  • No record of what you read, what verses you bookmark, what city you set, or which calculation method you use is transmitted off your device. (Those preferences and your last-read position live in a local SQLite file the app uses to remember where you were — they are never sent anywhere, and you can delete them all by uninstalling the app.)
  • No tracking across other apps or websites. No retargeting. No data sold to anyone.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, photo library, calendar, or health data accessed at any point.

What we do collect

Awaab uses Expo Application Services — the platform we build and ship the app with — for two narrow operational purposes. Both transmit only anonymous, aggregated signals:

  • expo-updates — over-the-air bug fix delivery. On launch, the app asks Expo whether a JavaScript bundle update is available for your installed app version. The request carries: your app version, runtime version, OS version, and an anonymous Expo install ID (randomly generated on first launch, not tied to your Apple/Google account or any device hardware identifier). No personal data is sent. Endpoint: u.expo.dev.
  • expo-insights — aggregate adoption metrics. On launch, the app records an anonymous app-launch event. The request carries: the same anonymous Expo install ID, OS version, app version, and basic diagnostic data (e.g. cold-start performance, JS errors if any). No personal data, no precise location, no usage details (we do not know which screens you visited, what you read, or what you searched). Endpoint: i.expo.dev.

The Expo install ID is reset every time you uninstall and reinstall the app. Expo Inc. is the data processor; their privacy practices are at expo.dev/privacy. We do not sell, share, or otherwise hand this data to advertisers, brokers, or any other party.

What is stored on your device

The following data lives only on your device, and you can delete it by uninstalling the app:

  • Settings — language, theme, calculation method, Asr school, time format, prayer-alert toggles, and Arabic font size.
  • Location coordinates — when granted, used only to compute prayer times. We never transmit your location.
  • City label — derived from your coordinates via on-device reverse-geocoding, or whatever city you manually entered.
  • Last-read position — the sūrah and verse you were reading, so you can resume.
  • Bookmarks — verses you have saved.
  • Cached prayer times — pre-computed for your location, refreshed daily on-device.

None of this is shared with us, with Apple or Google beyond what the OS already does for any installed app, or with anyone else.

Network traffic

The complete list of network requests Awaab makes:

  • u.expo.dev (Expo Updates) — checked on cold launch and at most once per session, to fetch over-the-air JavaScript fixes for your installed app version. Anonymous install ID + app/runtime version only.
  • i.expo.dev (Expo Insights) — anonymous launch event on cold launch. Anonymous install ID + OS/app version + basic diagnostic data.
  • App Store / Play Store (OS-level) — the operating system itself may check for binary updates. We do not control this; it is the same behavior as any other installed app.

That is the entire list. Awaab does not contact ad networks, third-party analytics services other than Expo Insights, third-party crash reporters, social-media SDKs, or any other server.

Third-party services

Awaab uses Expo Application Services (operated by Expo Inc.) as its sole third-party processor, for expo-updates and expo-insights as described above. Their privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy. Awaab transmits no data to any other third party.

The marketing site at elshazlyyy.github.io/Awaab-Public uses no analytics, no cookies, and no third-party scripts other than Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com); if you would prefer Google Fonts were self-hosted, please tell us and we will move them.

Data deletion

Because no data on our side is linked to you personally, deletion happens automatically:

  • On your device — uninstalling Awaab removes every setting, bookmark, last-read position, and cached prayer time. There is no cloud account to delete because there is no cloud account.
  • The anonymous Expo install ID is randomly generated locally on first launch. Uninstalling the app removes it; a future reinstall generates a fresh, unrelated ID. Expo retains the aggregated, non-personally-identifiable launch counts only.
  • If you would like Expo to investigate or remove any specific record from their backend, contact them at expo.dev/privacy with the date range; we are also happy to help — email us and we will assist.

Children's privacy

Awaab is rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play, and is suitable for all ages. The anonymous operational telemetry described above contains no personally identifying information about anyone, regardless of age. We do not knowingly target the app at children under 13, nor do we collect any information that could identify a child. The app is therefore compliant with COPPA (US), the GDPR's standards for children (EU/UK), and the equivalent provisions in other jurisdictions.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what data the app handles, we will update the date at the top of this page and ship the new policy with the corresponding app release. We will not change this policy silently.
