AGE SUITABILITY

Age suitability

LAST UPDATED · 25 MAY 2026

Rating

Awaab is rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play. The app is suitable for all ages, from young children learning to read the Qur'an to elderly users practising their daily prayer.

What is in the app

Awaab contains the full text of the Qur'an in Uthmānī Arabic script with an English translation (Saheeh International), prayer-time calculations, and a qibla compass. There is no other content. There are no:

  • Discussion forums, comments, or social features
  • Chat, messaging, or contact features
  • User-generated content of any kind
  • Advertisements
  • External links to third-party services or websites within the app
  • Profanity, violent imagery, or mature themes

For parents

You can hand your child a device running Awaab without worry. The app does not access the camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or any other personal data beyond location (which is used solely to compute prayer times and never leaves the device).

The reader's long-press menu includes Share, Copy, and Bookmark actions. Tapping Share opens the standard system share sheet — what happens after that is governed by the apps your child has installed.

Children's data

Awaab does not collect data from any user, including users under 13. This is explained more fully in our privacy policy. The app is compliant with COPPA, GDPR-K, and the equivalent regulations in other jurisdictions.

In-app purchases

There are no in-app purchases. The app is free, and there is no paid tier.
