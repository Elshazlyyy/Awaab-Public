Age Suitability

Safe for everyone, at any age.

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Awaab is a free Islamic companion app — a Quran reader, prayer times, and a qibla finder. It is designed to be a calm, trustworthy daily tool, and it is suitable for users of all ages.

The short version

  • Rated 4+ on the App Store and Everyone on Google Play — suitable for all ages.
  • No objectionable content of any kind.
  • No advertising — ever, anywhere in the app.
  • No user-generated content, no chat, no social features, no profiles.
  • No in-app purchases, no subscriptions — the app is free.
  • No data collection. Nothing leaves your phone.

Why Awaab is suitable for all ages

The content is the Quran and prayer tools

Awaab contains the Quran, calculated prayer times, and a qibla compass. There is no fictional content, no violence, no profanity, and no mature themes. The Quran text is presented faithfully and respectfully.

No advertising

Awaab shows no ads — ever. Children using the app will never see a banner, an interstitial, or a promoted product.

No way to be contacted by strangers

There are no forums, no comments, no profiles, no chat, no friend lists, and no way for one user of Awaab to reach another. The app has no server and no account system, so there is structurally no path for a stranger to message a child through it.

No purchases

Awaab is free, with no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. There is no checkout flow, no payment screen, and no way for a child to spend money inside the app.

No data collection

Awaab does not collect, store, or transmit personal data — see the privacy policy for the full details. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

For parents and guardians

If you are letting a child use Awaab, you can do so confidently. There is nothing in the app that needs supervising — no purchases to disable, no social settings to lock down, no ad filter to manage. The app simply opens to the Quran, prayer times, and qibla.
