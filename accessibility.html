Accessibility

Working to make Awaab usable for everyone.

Last updated: May 21, 2026

Awaab is a quiet, daily companion — and a daily companion is only useful if you can actually use it. We are committed to accessibility, and we want to be honest about where the app stands today and what we are still working on.

The short version

  • Awaab supports your phone's Dark Mode, with a manual light / dark / system toggle inside the app.
  • We are actively working to improve VoiceOver, Larger Text, color contrast, and reduced motion in upcoming releases.
  • If something is hard to use, please tell us — your feedback shapes the next update.

What works today

Your phone's appearance setting

Awaab follows your phone's appearance setting and switches between a warm cream-and-bronze light theme and a deep slate dark theme automatically. You can also override the system preference inside the app if you prefer to lock Awaab to light or dark independently of the rest of your phone.

Adjustable Arabic text size

The Quran reader has its own Arabic text size, independent of the system text size. So you can scale Arabic letterforms up or down without affecting the rest of the interface — a small win for low-vision readers of Arabic in particular.

Quiet motion on the qibla compass

The compass needle eases gently toward the Kaaba rather than snapping. We chose this for calm — it also helps anyone sensitive to abrupt motion.

What we are actively working on

The following areas are on our active roadmap. We are not claiming full support for them yet — we want you to know what to expect, and what we are still polishing.

VoiceOver and screen reader support

We are working to label buttons, controls, and Quran navigation in a way that VoiceOver and TalkBack can announce clearly. Reading the Quran with a screen reader should feel calm and predictable, and we are not there yet.

Larger Text (system text size)

Awaab honours the system text size in some places today, but not consistently across every screen. We are going through the app to make every label, button, and body of text honour your phone's Text Size setting.

Color contrast

The app's cream-and-bronze palette is part of what makes it feel calm, but we know some combinations may not yet meet contrast guidelines for low-vision readers. We are reviewing every screen and adjusting tones where they fall short.

Reduced motion

For people who prefer reduced motion, we are working to honour your phone's "Reduce Motion" setting throughout the app — softening transitions and animations where the system asks us to. This website already respects that setting.

How to send feedback

If something in Awaab is hard to use, slow to read, or confusing with assistive technology, we want to know. Specific feedback — even a single screen or a single button — helps more than you would expect.
