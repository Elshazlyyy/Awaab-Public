ACCESSIBILITY

Accessibility

LAST UPDATED · 29 MAY 2026

Our commitment

Awaab is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA across every screen, and reaches AAA for body text wherever it can be done without compromising the editorial voice. The app is intended to be usable by everyone, in every state, on every device.

Contrast & color

Body text passes WCAG AAA at 7:1 in both light and dark themes. Eyebrows and tertiary labels meet AA. Bronze, the only accent color, comes in two values: a slightly darker "text bronze" (#76592A light / #C9A86A dark) ensures small-cap captions stay readable at 9–11px.

Color never carries critical meaning alone. Every status the app shows is encoded with a shape, label, and position in addition to color. You can use Awaab with grayscale, deuteranopia, protanopia, or tritanopia color settings and lose no information.

Type & sizing

  • Dynamic Type is respected on iOS and Android. All text scales without truncation up to 200%.
  • Tap targets are at least 44×44 pt on iOS and 48×48 dp on Android.
  • Arabic Quranic text is rendered in Amiri Quran with line-height 1.95 so tashkeel (vowel marks) breathe.
  • Reader type controls let you scale the Arabic verse size up to about 42 pt and down to about 22 pt.

Motion

Awaab respects prefers-reduced-motion on both the app and this website. Every animation in the system has a documented instant-or-static fallback. The compass needle settles linearly rather than via spring; the long-press menu fades in rather than scaling; the marketing site's count-up does not run.

Screen readers

The app is tested against VoiceOver (iOS) and TalkBack (Android). Every interactive element carries an accessibility label in the user's chosen UI language, including the long-press menu, segmented controls, the qibla dial, the qibla direction marker, the bookmark toggle, the reader's back chevron, and every tab in the bottom navigation. Arabic verses are announced with their lang="ar" attribute so screen readers use the correct voice. Quranic Arabic ships bundled in Amiri Quran at every supported OS version, so tashkeel (vowel marks) render consistently on Android 8 and newer without falling back to the device's system font.

Keyboard & switch control

On iPad, every screen is navigable via external keyboard. Focus rings are visible and consistent (2px bronze outline, 3px offset). Switch Control users can advance through every interactive element in a sensible reading order — top-to-bottom on LTR screens, top-to-bottom mirrored on RTL.
