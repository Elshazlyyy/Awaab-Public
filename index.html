What it does

Three companions. One peaceful app.

Each feature works offline. Nothing leaves your device.

Quran reader

The full Mushaf, bundled with the app and indexed in a local SQLite database for fast, gentle reading.

  • All 114 sūrahs
  • Uthmānī, IndoPak, or Naskh script
  • English translation alongside
  • Adjustable Arabic text size
  • Tafsīr · Bookmarks · Verse sharing

Prayer times

Daily prayer times calculated on your device from your location, with quiet local notifications when each prayer enters.

  • Fajr · Dhuhr · Asr · Maghrib · Isha
  • Plus Sunrise, Midnight, last third
  • Six calculation methods
  • Standard or Hanafi Asr
  • Hijri and Gregorian dates

Qibla finder

Hold your phone flat and turn — the compass aligns toward the Kaaba, guided by the device's motion sensor.

  • Bronze marker for the qibla
  • Smooth, low-jitter needle
  • Works offline anywhere on Earth
  • Subtle haptic feedback on lock-on
  • Heading never leaves the device
Crafted details

The small things, looked after.

Awaab is a quiet utility — and quiet apps live in their details.

01 · Reading

Bookmarks that stay yours

Saved verses live in a local database on your phone. Nothing is uploaded — and nothing can be lost to a server you don't control.

02 · Reading

Three Arabic scripts

Choose Uthmānī for the classical mushaf, IndoPak for South-Asian reading, or Naskh for a cleaner sans feel. Switch anytime.

03 · Reading

Tafsīr and translation

Read the English translation alongside Arabic, with Tafsīr context tucked one tap away. Stays out of the way until you ask.

04 · Reading

Share a verse, beautifully

Tap to render a verse as a calm typographic card and send it via the system share sheet — no Awaab account or server involved.

05 · Prayer

Six calculation methods

Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, Egyptian Authority, Karachi, and Tehran — pick the method your community follows.

06 · Prayer

Asr madhab toggle

Standard or Hanafi. The calculation respects your school of jurisprudence, and remembers it across launches.

07 · Prayer

Hijri calendar, today

Both Gregorian and Hijri dates appear on the Today screen — the Islamic calendar isn't an afterthought, it's part of the home view.

08 · System

Follows your system theme

Awaab respects your phone's appearance setting — cream and bronze by day, deep slate and warm cream by night, with no toggle to remember.
