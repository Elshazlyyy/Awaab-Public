Three companions. One peaceful app.

Each feature works offline. Nothing leaves your phone.

Quran reader

The full Mushaf, bundled with the app. Read in Arabic with optional English alongside it — calm typography, no clutter, no connection required.

Prayer times

Accurate daily times calculated on your device from your location. Gentle local notifications when each prayer enters — never a noisy push from a server.

Qibla finder

Hold your phone and turn — the compass guides you to the Kaaba using your device's motion sensor. Everything stays on-device, including your heading.

Privacy by design

Your data never leaves your device.

No accounts. No analytics. No ads. No third-party trackers. Awaab is a quiet, local app — and that is the whole story.
