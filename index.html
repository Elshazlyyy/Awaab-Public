WHAT IT DOES

Qur'an reader

The full mushaf in Uthmānī script, with verse-by-verse English translation. Search across name, number, and meaning.

  • 114 surahs, offline
  • Uthmānī script (Madinah)
  • English translation built in
  • Bookmark any verse
  • Long-press to share or copy

Prayer times

Six calculation methods, auto-detected from your country. Asr school you choose. High-latitude rules where they matter.

  • Umm al-Qura, MWL, ISNA, Egyptian, Karachi, Tehran
  • Standard or Hanafi Asr
  • Per-prayer notifications
  • No badge dot on your home screen

Qibla finder

A real magnetic compass that points to the Kaʿbah. Subtle haptic when you are aligned. Works without a network.

  • Magnetic + true-north corrected
  • Bearing in degrees
  • Distance to Mecca
  • Figure-8 calibration prompt
  • Works offline forever

CRAFTED DETAILS

01

Reading

Uthmānī script set in Amiri Quran, with tashkeel breathing room (line-height 1.95) so vowel marks never crowd.

02

Type

Italic Cormorant Garamond for display; Inter Tight for body. Italic is Latin-only — Arabic stays upright.

03

Bronze

A single bronze accent. Two values: one for ornaments, one — slightly darker — for accent text. WCAG AA at every size.

04

Motion

Open the long-press menu and the preview emerges at 92% scale, settling with a 3% overshoot. Felt, not noticed.

05

Privacy

No accounts. No ads. No advertising trackers. No PII. Anonymous Expo telemetry only — for OTA bug fixes and aggregate adoption. Full disclosure.

06

Offline

Every prayer time, every verse, every bookmark — computed and stored on-device. The plane mode test passes.

07

Bilingual

Arabic and English are equally first-class. The Arabic surface was designed natively, not flipped from English.

08

System

Light and dark, automatic. Respects your dynamic type, your reduced-motion preference, your reduced-transparency setting.

QUESTIONS

Does Awaab work offline?

Yes — every prayer time, every verse, every bookmark is computed and stored on your device. We don't need the network.

Will you ever add accounts?

No. The app does not have user accounts, and it never will. Your data stays where you put it.

How is location used?

Only to compute prayer times. On iOS the city lookup stays entirely on your device; on Android the OS performs one Google Play Services geocoder lookup to turn your coordinates into a city name. You can also override it manually with any city.

Why no ads?

Because adhan and ads do not belong on the same screen. We support the project by other means.

Is the translation reliable?

We use Marmaduke Pickthall's classic English translation (public domain), long respected for its fidelity to the Arabic.

What translation does it use?

Awaab uses Marmaduke Pickthall's English translation (public domain) and the Noble Qur’an Encyclopedia (quranenc.com) Arabic text (CC BY-SA 4.0). Prayer-time math by adhan. See full licenses or view source on GitHub.

PRIVACY PROMISE

Your personal content stays on your device.

No accounts. No ads. No advertising trackers. No data sold to anyone. The app does transmit a small set of anonymous operational telemetry via Expo so we can ship bug fixes — no PII, no record of what you read. Read the full policy — it is short.
