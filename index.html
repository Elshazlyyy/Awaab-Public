What it does

Three companions. One peaceful app.

Each feature works without a connection. Nothing leaves your phone.

Quran reader

The full Mushaf, ready to read in any moment — no connection needed, ever.

  • All 114 sūrahs included
  • Uthmānī, IndoPak, or Naskh script
  • English translation alongside
  • Adjustable Arabic text size
  • Tafsīr · Bookmarks · Verse sharing

Prayer times

Accurate daily times for wherever you are — with quiet reminders as each prayer enters.

  • Fajr · Dhuhr · Asr · Maghrib · Isha
  • Plus Sunrise, Midnight, the last third
  • Six calculation methods
  • Standard or Hanafi Asr
  • Hijri and Gregorian dates

Qibla finder

Hold your phone flat and turn — the compass eases gently toward the Kaaba, wherever in the world you stand.

  • A bronze marker for the qibla
  • Smooth, never jittery
  • A gentle vibration when aligned
  • Works anywhere on Earth
  • Your heading stays on your phone
Crafted details

The small things, looked after.

Awaab is a quiet utility — and quiet apps live in their details.

01 · Reading

Bookmarks that stay yours

Saved verses stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded — and nothing can be lost.

02 · Reading

Three Arabic scripts

Choose Uthmānī for the classical mushaf, IndoPak for South-Asian reading, or Naskh for a cleaner sans feel.

03 · Reading

Tafsīr and translation

Read the English translation beside the Arabic, with Tafsīr context one tap away.

04 · Reading

Share a verse, beautifully

Turn any verse into a calm typographic card and share it with friends — no account required.

05 · Prayer

Six calculation methods

From Umm al-Qura to Karachi to Tehran — whichever your community follows, anywhere in the world.

06 · Prayer

Asr madhab toggle

Standard or Hanafi. Awaab respects your school of jurisprudence and remembers it.

07 · Prayer

Hijri calendar, today

Both Gregorian and Hijri dates appear on the home screen — the Islamic calendar is right there with you.

08 · System

Follows your phone's theme

Cream and bronze by day, deep slate and warm cream by night. No toggle to remember.
